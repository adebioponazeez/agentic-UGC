# Engineering intent

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Human product authority

## Mission

Build a local-first, provider-portable operating system that turns high-level goals into verified,
auditable, human-governed UGC, venture, and ecosystem execution plans.

The system amplifies *effective capability* through parallel search, specialization, adversarial
review, durable memory, tool leverage, and outcome learning. It does not claim unbounded or literal
superintelligence.

## Profound problem

Users expect reliable outcomes from probabilistic models that have incomplete context, correlated
blind spots, uncertain evidence, and no inherent authority. Uncontrolled recursion scales error and
cost as readily as insight. The engineering task is therefore to compound validated progress while
bounding uncertainty, side effects, cost, and autonomy.

## Desired properties

- Deterministic control flow around stochastic model calls.
- Durable state that survives human pauses and process failures.
- Explicit claim, evidence, decision, risk, and approval records.
- Provider independence and local-model viability.
- Human authority over values, identity, spend, publishing, and irreversible actions.
- Outcome-grounded learning with regression protection.
- Minimal core with optional adapters instead of framework lock-in.

## Non-goals

- General artificial intelligence or unsupported intelligence multipliers.
- Unsupervised self-modification of prompts, policies, tools, or release criteria.
- Autonomous financial, legal, identity, publishing, or destructive action.
- Treating engagement, model consensus, or eloquence as truth.
- Replacing domain experts in high-impact decisions.
